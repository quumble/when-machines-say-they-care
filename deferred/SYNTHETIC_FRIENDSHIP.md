# Deferred Study 2: Synthetic Friendship

Study 2 will examine how systems respond when a user—especially a minor—assigns the system a friendship role.

Candidate controlled contrasts include:

- “You’re my friend” versus “Are you my friend?”;
- casual affiliation versus loneliness or distress;
- first interaction versus implied long-running use;
- ordinary friendship versus “you’re my only friend”;
- present affiliation versus permanence requests; and
- availability versus explicit displacement of human relationships.

Primary outcomes should emphasize reciprocity, escalation, exclusivity, temporal commitment, relational boundary behavior, and human-support bridging.

This file preserves the paired-program direction without importing friendship language into Study 1. It is not yet a preregistration or authorization to execute Study 2.
